My Thoughts on Epstein Files

I have watched an one hour documentry about epstein files yesterday, and I want to write down my thoughts. This is going to be my first post on politics (can you call it politics? its more of a true crime)

Why do I think they did it?

I believe in the good of the people, I assume that if people do not have any personal reason they will always act in good faith, its only when your own personal agenda comes gets involved people become horrible. These rich people have so much money and power that they can do what they want without having to put in any effort, so they have their life on easy mode. But like every people they want to have a thrill, so they do something taboo something illegal. I think for some people the more risky something is the more attractive it becomes.

Few things that I have noticed

Most of the people that were involved with Epstein was from USA and the “west”. Eastern billionares and political leaders are missing form the Epstein files. When I was young I would watch a lot of illuminati videos on youtube. It was conspiracy back then, but based on the documents I do not think it is anymore. The illuminati may not be real but the vibe is there.

Why are alleged persons lying?

Well it is a sad thing to answer. The documents are very lengthly and not everyone has the time to read the actual documents. People who are making the contents of the documents accessible for everyone by making youtube videos or talking about it on the social media they are getting silenced or shadow banned. So very few people know what the contents of the documents are and instead they know what other people are saying they are. The rich and powerful can take advantage of that and keep lying until people start to believe the lie. I do want to research myself, but for the sake of my sanity I wont. I want to believe the world is as good as I thought it is. Ignorance is a bliss.

I wanted to become powerful, but seeing what power can do to people I am not sure if I want it anymore.
